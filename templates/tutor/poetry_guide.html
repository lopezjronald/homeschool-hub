The poetic slash. {{ poetic_slash }}

The original pages are on her pages

Each section shows the guide's own pages right above the work — the worked example in the author's handwriting, the crossings-out, the slashes drawn in by hand. That is the part of this guide an app cannot recreate, so she reads the real thing and the app does what paper can't:

  • The syllable grid. One line per line of the form, its target beside it, and a live count that turns green on target. The counter is an estimate — English syllable counting by rule is approximate — so her own clapped-out count wins, and nothing blocks.
  • The four steps, kept. Sentence → edit to count → poetic slash → final poem, each its own answer, so you can see the path and not just the poem.
