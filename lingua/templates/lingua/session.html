{% endif %} {# The routine. Deliberately three steps and 20 minutes — a longer plan is one you abandon. #}
  1. 5 min Review Yesterday's dictado words. Say each one, she writes it. No new material.
  2. 10 min Read together A library book or a story she knows. You read a page, she reads a page. Point at pictures and use the questions below.
  3. 5 min Write Copy the lines on the sheet, then the dictado. On paper — handwriting is what builds the spelling memory.
You're the facilitator, not the expert. You do not need to be fluent for this to work — you need the routine to happen, in Spanish, most days. Tap any phrase to hear it, then say it yourself.
