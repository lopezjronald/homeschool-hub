What a week looks like here

  1. You read the book aloud — twice, per the publisher. The second listen is when the traits land.
  2. She opens the week — one page with the ten words and what they mean at the top.
  3. She finishes ten sentences about that person, choosing from those ten words.
  4. She writes three things that amazed her — by hand, with her finger or the pen. Her handwriting shows up in your work browser and prints in the charter report.
  5. Her poster fills in — those ten words colour in when she turns the week in. No colouring pencils needed.
Worth knowing: weeks 5–10 were transcribed from a guide that prints its definitions under decorative artwork, so parts came through garbled and were reconstructed. Those are worth a glance against your printed copy before she reaches them. Also, week 9 repeats the publisher's own error that Grace Hopper was promoted to Admiral after her death — she was promoted in 1985 and died in 1992.
